The Process

From inspection to done

Step 1

Free Inspection

We walk the roof, photograph everything, and hand you written findings. No cost and no obligation.

Step 2

Written Fixed Proposal

An itemized, fixed-price proposal in writing. On an insurance claim we build the scope to match what your policy owes you.

Step 3

Schedule the Work

Pick a window that suits your household. Your foreman confirms the schedule in writing before the first pallet arrives.

Step 4

Perform the Work

The crew arrives and the roof goes on — a dedicated foreman, photos to your phone every day, and a magnet-rolled yard before we leave.

Step 5 · Optional

Maintenance & Care

Optional, and worth it: annual tune-ups, moss and debris removal, and priority scheduling after storms.
Get Started Starts with a free inspection, not a sales call.

Materials

What we install

Most insurance claims replace like for like, so what you have is usually what you get. We fit both, and we will tell you which one your policy actually owes you.

Asphalt shingle

IKO architectural shingle

Our shingle of choice: family-owned, manufactured in North America, and specified for Texas wind and hail. Nine out of ten homes we re-roof are shingle, and the profile and color are matched to what came off.

Explore IKO shingles ↗

Metal

Standing seam and metal panel

Concealed-fastener standing seam and exposed-fastener panel, formed to your roofline. It costs more up front and lasts a great deal longer, which is why so much of the Hill Country is under it.
